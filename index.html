About Me

Third-year EECS student at UC Berkeley focused on digital design and computer architecture — from RTL to silicon. I work in SystemVerilog, Verilog, and Chisel, with experience in ASIC design, verification, and RISC-V microarchitecture.

On campus, I am an undergraduate researcher in the TAFLab and the Lead Electrical Engineer for Bike Builders of Berkeley, and I tutor EECS coursework for Engineering Student Services. Previously, I was a Hardware Engineering Intern at Pano AI, and in summer 2025 a Hardware Engineering Intern at Vision IV.
